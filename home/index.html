Make an informed choice

Three checks before your first reading

01 / YOUR QUESTION

Match the specialty

Write one specific question. Look for an advisor whose profile describes that topic and a style you feel comfortable with. A high overall rating does not automatically mean a good fit.

02 / YOUR FORMAT

Choose how to talk

Chat gives you time to write and revisit the conversation. Phone can suit a natural back-and-forth. Video adds face-to-face contact. Confirm which options the provider actually offers.

03 / YOUR BUDGET

Set a session limit

A per-minute price is only part of the cost. Decide how long you want to spend, check introductory terms, and agree on a stopping point before the session starts.

How to read the ratings

More context.
Better decisions.

A score can help narrow your choices. It cannot guarantee a reading’s accuracy or predict your experience.

Read the number behind the score

A score from a few ratings is less informative than the same score with a larger review history. Compare both, then read the profile details.

Separate profile facts from promises

Specialties and skills describe the service offered. They do not establish that a claimed psychic ability has been scientifically verified.

Keep your decisions in your hands

Use readings for personal reflection or entertainment. Seek qualified professionals for medical, legal, or financial decisions. Avoid pressure to pay for guaranteed outcomes or curse removal.

Before you connect

Your questions, answered

How should I compare psychic ratings?

Look at the score together with the number of ratings, the listed specialties, and the price. A rating records feedback; it is not proof of psychic accuracy. Review the full profile before choosing.

Can I find a reader for a specific topic?

Yes. Browse the specialty links above to see profiles associated with your topic. Check each advisor’s description for the questions they cover and their approach to a session.

How much should I budget for a reading?

Multiply the listed per-minute rate by your planned session length. A 15-minute reading at $3 per minute has a base cost of $45. Confirm the actual rate, any offer conditions, and additional charges with the provider.

Are the listed prices and availability live?

Treat directory prices and availability as reference information. They may have changed since the profile was recorded. The provider’s current booking page is the place to confirm them.

What are warning signs when choosing an advisor?

Be cautious about guaranteed results, urgent demands for more money, claims that you must pay to remove a curse, or pressure to replace professional advice. You can stop a session that makes you uncomfortable.
